What I do

Customer success is support with a longer view. I keep customers onboarded, adopting the product and renewing, and I catch problems before they become churn.

Onboarding and adoption

I onboard new customers, run training and check ins, and track adoption until the product is part of their daily work. A good first 30 days decides whether a customer stays.

Retention and renewals

I spot at risk accounts early from usage, tickets and sentiment, then act on them. I run renewal conversations with honest value reviews, not pressure, and I win back customers who were ready to leave.

SLA and escalation management

I own escalations end to end, keep customers updated while a fix is in progress, and manage SLAs so urgent issues never sit silent. Clear communication is half of customer success.

CSAT, NPS and health checks

I track satisfaction and health scores, run regular check ins, and turn feedback into action. When customers see their feedback change the product, trust grows.

Working with product and engineering

I carry the voice of the customer into product and engineering, with real examples and real numbers. I have worked directly with founders and product teams to fix the issues customers hit most.

Playbooks, SOPs and QA

I write success playbooks, SOPs and QA checks so every customer gets the same high standard, no matter who on the team handles them. Consistency is what makes success scalable.
